# Expose XIII §5 (gentle) — Abhyankar variants and the gentle SGA closing

<!-- source: XIII.5 -->

## What this file is about

Abhyankar's lemma is a recipe for untangling a controlled cover. Given a cover that spreads out along a thin locus of
forbidden points, one can adjoin roots to the base and obtain a new cover that is clean — unramified everywhere — over
the new base. The recipe works only for the controlled flavour of spreading-out, the tame one. The opposite flavour,
wild, is the bad ramification that the prime characteristic of the base field forces, and no construction straightens it
out. An earlier file proved a local form. The geometric setting now needs a family version, with the roots adjoined
simultaneously in every direction.

The relative lemma also closes the thirteen-chapter tour. The gentle SGA project read Grothendieck's first seminar in a
voice accessible to a working algebraic geometer rather than to a specialist. The closing remarks point forward to the
later seminars.

File 51 (X.3) proved Abhyankar's lemma in the local form that file 50 (X.2.6) needs. File 62 (XIII.2.4) needed a sharper
*relative* version: an étale covering of $U$ tamely ramified along an NC divisor $D$ extends, after a Kummer base
change, to an étale covering of all of $X$. This is the **absolute Abhyankar lemma** XIII.5.2, with relative refinements
XIII.5.3–XIII.5.6. File 64 reproduces all the variants in the gentle voice and uses them to close the gentle SGA
project.

The structure of the file is: XIII.5.1 (the local Kummer cover), XIII.5.2 (the absolute Abhyankar lemma), XIII.5.3,
XIII.5.3.0 (the strictly-local picture and the resulting tame fundamental-group calculation), XIII.5.4 (the smoothness
statement of the relative Kummer model), XIII.5.5 (the relative Abhyankar lemma proper), XIII.5.6 (the universal tame
covering of a strict-local NC neighbourhood). The closing section walks through the thirteen-exposé gentle SGA tour and
points forward to SGA 4 / SGA 4½ / SGA 5.

## §XIII.5.1 — Local Kummer at a regular NC point

<!-- source: XIII.5.1 -->

*This section says:* if $X = \operatorname{Spec} A$ is a regular local scheme with $D = \sum div(f_{i})$ cut out by part
of a regular system of parameters, and $n_{i}$ are integers prime to the residual characteristic $p$, then the explicit
Kummer cover $X' = X[T_{1}, \cdots, T_{r}] / (T^{n_{i}}_{i} - f_{i})$ is regular, the boundary $\sum div(T_{i})$ is
again NC, and $U' = U \times_{X} X'$ is a connected tame étale cover of $U$ relative to $D$.

### XIII.5.1 — Proposition.

<!-- source: XIII.5.1 -->

Let $X = \operatorname{Spec} A$ be a regular local scheme, and let

```text
D = ∑_{1 ≤ i ≤ r} div(f_i)
```

be an NC divisor where the $f_{i} \in \mathfrak{m}_{A}$ are part of a regular system of parameters of $A$. Let
$n_{i} \geq 0$ be integers and put

```text
X' = X[T_1, …, T_r] / (T_1^{n_1} − f_1, …, T_r^{n_r} − f_r),
U' = U ×_X X'.
```

Then $X'$ is regular, and $U'$ is the complement in $X'$ of the NC divisor $\sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(T_{i})$. If the
$n_{i}$ are prime to the residual characteristic $p$ of $X$, then $U' \to U$ is a connected étale cover of $U$, tamely
ramified relative to $D$ (XIII.2.3 c).

*Proof.* $X'$ is the spectrum of a local ring $A'$ whose maximal ideal is generated by $T_{1}, \cdots, T_{r}$. We may
suppose $r = \dim(A)$. Since $A'$ is finite and flat over $A$ (it is a finite product of monomial extensions, and Kummer
extensions of a regular local ring by uniformiser radicals stay finite flat), its dimension is again $r$. Hence $A'$ is
regular (EGA `0_IV` 17.1.1), and the $T_{i}$ form a regular system of parameters of $A'$.

If the $n_{i}$ are prime to $p$, every $f_{i}$ is invertible on $U$, so the equations $T^{n_{i}}_{i} = f_{i}$ define an
étale extension on $U$ by I.7.4 (file 11). The étale cover $U' \to U$ is tame relative to $D$: at the generic point
$x_{i}$ of $V(f_{i})$ and its strict localisation $\bar{R} = (\mathcal{O}_{X, x_{i}})^{sh}$ with fraction field
$\bar{K}$, the $\bar{K}$-algebra representing $U'|_{\bar{K}}$ is obtained from the field
$\bar{K}[T_{i}] / (T^{n_{i}}_{i} - f_{i})$ by making an unramified extension; it is therefore tame over $\bar{R}$ (the
inertia at $T_{i}$ is cyclic of order $n_{i}$, prime to $p$).

Connectedness of $U'$ follows from the irreducibility of $T^{n_{i}}_{i} - f_{i}$ over the fraction field of the regular
local ring $A$ localised at $f_{i}$, when $n_{i}$ is prime to $p$ (a standard Kummer-theoretic fact).

Slogan: **a tame Kummer extension along the components of an NC divisor stays inside the étale category on the
complement; conversely (XIII.5.2), every tame étale cover *is* a Kummer extension.**

## §XIII.5.2 — The absolute Abhyankar lemma

<!-- source: XIII.5.2 -->

### XIII.5.2 — Proposition.

<!-- source: XIII.5.2 -->

Let $X$ be a regular local scheme, $D = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(f_{i})$ an NC divisor with the $f_{i}$ part of a
regular system of parameters (as in XIII.5.1). Let $Y = Supp D$, $U = X - Y$, and let $V \to U$ be an étale cover tamely
ramified relative to $D$. For each $i$, let $x_{i}$ be the generic point of $V(f_{i})$; then $\mathcal{O}_{X, x_{i}}$ is
a DVR with fraction field $K_{i}$, and

```text
V|_{K_i}  =  Spec( ∏_{j ∈ J_i} L_j ),
```

where the $L_{j}$ are finite separable extensions of $K_{i}$. Let $n_{j}$ denote the order of the inertia group of a
Galois extension generated by $L_{j}$, and let $n_{i}$ be the lcm of the $n_{j}$ as $j$ ranges over $J_{i}$. With

```text
X' = X[T_1, …, T_r] / (T_1^{n_1} − f_1, …, T_r^{n_r} − f_r),
U' = U ×_X X',  V' = V ×_X X',
```

the étale cover $V' \to U'$ extends uniquely, up to unique isomorphism, to an étale cover of $X'$, and the $n_{i}$ are
prime to the residual characteristic $p$ of $X$.

*Slogan.* **Every tame étale cover of $U$ becomes unramified after the Kummer base change $X' \to X$ determined by the
inertia exponents at the boundary components.**

### Proof of XIII.5.2.

#### Uniqueness.

Since $X'$ is normal (XIII.5.1), an étale cover of $X'$ extending $V'$ is isomorphic to the normalisation of $X'$ in the
fibre of $V'$ at the generic point of $X'$ (I.10.2 of file 12). So uniqueness up to unique iso is automatic.

#### Existence: étale-local reduction.

Let $\bar{x}'$ be a geometric point of $Y' = V(T_{1}) \cup \cdots \cup V(T_{r}) \subseteq X'$. Write $\bar{X}'$ for the
strict localisation of $X'$ at $\bar{x}'$, $\bar{V}' = V' \times_{X'} \bar{X}'$, etc. By descent and uniqueness, it
suffices to show that for every such $\bar{x}'$, the étale cover $\bar{V}'$ of `Ū'` extends to an étale cover of
$\bar{X}'$.

#### Extension across codim $\geq 2$.

By [`sga-2-xiv-1-11`](appendix-black-boxes.md#sga-2-xiv-1-11) (SGA 2 XIV 1.11), an étale cover of an open subset of a
regular scheme $\bar{X}'$ that contains every point of codimension $\leq 1$ extends uniquely to all of $\bar{X}'$. So we
may restrict to geometric points $\bar{x}'$ *projecting to a maximal point of $Y'$* — i.e. to the generic point of a
single irreducible component $V(T_{i_{0}})$.

#### Extension at a maximal point.

At a geometric point $\bar{x}'$ above a maximal point of $Y'$, $\bar{X}'_{red}$ is the spectrum of a DVR (the local ring
at the generic point of $V(T_{i_{0}})$ after strict henselisation). The étale cover $\bar{V}'$ of `Ū'` is tame at that
DVR (since $V$ was tame at $X_{x_{i_{0}}}$, and we have absorbed the inertia exponent $n_{i_{0}}$ into the Kummer base
change). At a strictly-local DVR, an étale cover of the punctured spectrum that is tame at the missing point extends to
the whole spectrum — this is file 51's X.3.6.

This completes the construction of the extension.

#### The $n_{i}$ are prime to $p$.

Suppose for contradiction $p | n_{i_{0}}$ for some $i_{0}$. After replacing $X$ by

```text
X[T_{i_0}, T_i]_{i ≠ i_0} / (T_{i_0}^{n_{i_0} / p} − f_{i_0}, T_i^{n_i} − f_i),
```

we are reduced to the case $X' = X[T] / (T^{p} - f_{i_{0}})$. The argument above gives an extension $W'$ of $V'$ to $X'$
(over $X$ we may suppose strictly local). Let $Z = V(p) \subseteq X$ and $Z_{1} = Z \cap X_{f_{1}}$; `Z_1` is a
non-empty open of $Z$. The étale cover $V'$ of $U'$ extends to an étale cover $W'$ of $X'$ (already established). Let
$W''_{1}, W''_{2}$ be the inverse images of $W'$ by the two projections `X'' = X' ×_X X' ⇉ X'`, and consider the descent
isomorphism $u : W''_{1}|_{U''} \to W''_{2}|_{U''}$. We must show $u$ extends to an `X''`-morphism
$W''_{1} \to W''_{2}$, which would then be descent data on $W'$ for $X' \to X$ and would force $V$ to extend to $X$.

Let $Z'' = X'' \times_{X} Z$, $Z''_{1} = X'' \times_{X} Z_{1}$. The morphism $Z'' \to Z$ is *radicial* (because
$X'' \to X$ is, modulo $p$), so there is an isomorphism $v : W''_{1}|_{Z''} \to W''_{2}|_{Z''}$ extending
$u|_{Z''_{1}}$. Since $X$ is henselian,
$\operatorname{Hom}_{X''}(W''_{1}, W''_{2}) \simeq \operatorname{Hom}_{Z''}(W''_{1}|_{Z''}, W''_{2}|_{Z''})$, so $v$
lifts to a morphism $w : W''_{1} \to W''_{2}$. The subscheme of `X''` on which $u$ and $w$ agree is both open and closed
(a closed-and-open subset of `X''`), and it contains $Z''_{1}$, hence is all of `X''`. This shows $V$ extends to $X$,
hence $n_{i_{0}} = 1$, contradicting $p | n_{i_{0}}$.

This completes the proof.

Slogan: **the absolute Abhyankar lemma is the codim-2 extension theorem of SGA 2 XIV plus X.3.6 plus a henselian-
descent step; the prime-to-$p$ conclusion comes from a clean contradiction at the radicial closed fibre.**

## §XIII.5.3, XIII.5.3.0 — Strictly-local picture and $\pi^{tame}_{1}$ of a strict-local NC neighbourhood

<!-- source: XIII.5.3 -->

### XIII.5.3.0 — Tame coverings of a strictly-local NC neighbourhood.

<!-- source: XIII.5.3.0 -->

Resume the hypotheses and notation of XIII.5.2, and assume moreover that $X$ is *strictly local*. Then every connected
tame étale cover of $U$ relative to $D$ is a quotient of a Kummer cover of the form

```text
U' = U[T_1, …, T_r] / (T_1^{n_1} − f_1, …, T_r^{n_r} − f_r),
```

with the $n_{i}$ prime to $p$. Let $\mu_{n} \subseteq X$ be the group of $n$-th roots of unity (a finite étale $X$-group
scheme, since $X$ is strictly local). The group of $U$-automorphisms of $U'$ is then

$$
\mu_{n_{1}} \times \mu_{n_{2}} \times \cdots \times \mu_{n_{r}},
$$

an $n_{i}$-th root of unity $\xi_{i}$ acting on $U'$ by $T_{i} \mapsto \xi_{i} T_{i}$.

### XIII.5.3 — Corollary ($\pi^{tame}_{1}$ of a strict-local NC neighbourhood).

<!-- source: XIII.5.3 -->

Let $X$ be a strictly local regular scheme of residual characteristic $p \geq 0$,
$D = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(f_{i})$ an NC divisor, $U = X - Supp D$. Define

```text
Ũ  :=  lim_{(n_i)}  U[T_1, …, T_r] / (T_1^{n_1} − f_1, …, T_r^{n_r} − f_r),
```

the projective limit over the filtered ordered set (under divisibility) of tuples $(n_{i})$ of positive integers prime
to $p$. Then `Ũ` is a **universal tame étale cover of $U$** relative to $D$, and

```text
π_1^tame(U)  ≃  ∏_{ℓ ≠ p}  ℤ_ℓ(1)^r       (canonical iso),
```

where $\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1) = \lim_{n > 0} \mu_{\ell^{n}}$ (with the limit taken over powers of $\ell$ prime to $p$).
Non-canonically, $\pi^{tame}_{1}(U) \simeq (\Pi_{\ell \neq p} \mathbb{Z}_{\ell})^{r}$.

*Proof.* By XIII.5.3.0 every connected tame étale cover of $U$ is a quotient of a finite-Kummer cover
$U[T_{i}] / (T^{n_{i}}_{i} - f_{i})$; passing to the limit identifies `Ũ` with the universal tame cover. The
$U$-automorphism calculation in XIII.5.3.0 identifies
$\operatorname{Aut}_{U}(\tilde{U}) = \prod \lim_{n} \mu_{n} = \prod_{\ell \neq p} \mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1)$, and there are
$r$ factors (one per component of the NC divisor).

The non-canonical iso follows by choosing a uniformiser-by-uniformiser identification
$\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1) \simeq \mathbb{Z}_{\ell}$ (depends on a choice of primitive roots of unity).

Slogan for XIII.5.3: **strict-locally at an NC point, the tame fundamental group is
$\Pi_{\ell \neq p} \mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1)^{r}$, one factor per boundary component, generated by the Kummer-cover
monodromy.**

## §XIII.5.4 — Smoothness of the relative Kummer model

<!-- source: XIII.5.4 -->

### XIII.5.4 — Proposition.

<!-- source: XIII.5.4 -->

Let $f : X \to S$ be a morphism, $D = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(f_{i})$ an NC divisor relative to $S$ (XIII.5.4's
hypothesis is the strict-NC version of XIII.2.1.0). For a point $x$ of $Y = Supp D$ let $I(x) = { i : f_{i}(x) = 0}$.
Suppose $V((f_{i})_{i \in I(x)})$ is smooth over $S$ of codimension `#I(x)` in $X$. Let $U = X - Y$. Fix $x \in Y$, set
$X_{1} = \operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}_{X, x}$, $U_{1} = U \times_{X} X_{1}$, integers $(n_{i})_{i \in I(x)}$, and

```text
X' = X[T_i]_{i ∈ I(x)} / (T_i^{n_i} − f_i).
```

Let $x' \in X'$ be the unique point above $x$. Then $X'$ is *smooth over $S$ at $x'$*. If the $n_{i}$ are prime to the
residual characteristic $p$ of $\kappa(x)$, then $U'_{1} = U_{1} \times_{X} X'$ is a *connected tame étale cover of
`U_1`* relative to `S_1`, where `S_1` is the image of `X_1` in $S$ (XIII.2.1.1).

*Proof.* Set $s = f(x)$. The geometric fibre $X'_{\bar{s}}$ is regular at $x'$ by XIII.5.1 (the absolute statement
applied to the regular local scheme $X_{\bar{s}}$). $X'$ is flat over $S$ in a neighbourhood of $Y$ (the Kummer model is
a finite flat cover; its flatness propagates from the absolute case via étale-flat base change). Regularity of the
geometric fibre + flatness ⇒ smoothness (EGA IV 12.1.6).

If the $n_{i}$ are prime to $p$, étaleness of $U'_{1} \to U_{1}$ is I.7.4 (file 11). The tameness is checked fibre by
fibre using XIII.5.1. Connectedness of $U'_{1}$ follows from SGA 4 XVI 3.2
([`sga-4-xvi-base-change-smooth`](appendix-black-boxes.md#sga-4-xvi-base-change-smooth)).

## §XIII.5.5 — The relative Abhyankar lemma

<!-- source: XIII.5.5 -->

### XIII.5.5 — Proposition (relative Abhyankar).

<!-- source: XIII.5.5 -->

Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme, $D = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(f_{i})$ an NC divisor relative to $S$ as in XIII.5.4. Let
$Y = Supp D$, $U = X - Y$, $x \in Y$, $X_{1} = X_{(x)}$ the strict localisation at a geometric point above $x$,
$U_{1} = U \times_{X} X_{1}$, and let `V_1` be an étale cover of `U_1`. Suppose that for every maximal point $s$ of $S$,
the restriction $V_{1}|_{\bar{s}}$ is tamely ramified over $X_{1}|_{\bar{s}}$ relative to $\bar{s}$. Then there exist
integers $n_{i}$ ($i \in I(x)$) prime to the residual characteristic $p$ of $\kappa(x)$, such that, with

```text
X'_1 = X_1 [T_i]_{i ∈ I(x)} / (T_i^{n_i} − f_i),
U'_1 = U_1 ×_{X_1} X'_1,
V'_1 = V_1 ×_{X_1} X'_1,
```

the étale cover $V'_{1} \to U'_{1}$ extends *uniquely up to unique iso* to an étale cover of $X'_{1}$. In particular
`V_1` is tamely ramified over `X_1` relative to $S$.

*Slogan.* **The absolute Abhyankar lemma XIII.5.2 promoted to the relative $S$-setting: tameness over every maximal
point of $S$ implies tameness over $S$, with a Kummer base change controlled by inertia at each boundary component.**

### Proof of XIII.5.5.

We may suppose $S$ is local noetherian with closed point $f(x)$.

#### Choosing the $n_{i}$.

For each maximal point $s$ of $S$ and each $i \in I(x)$, let $x_{i, s}$ be the generic point of the closed subset
$V(f_{i})$ of the fibre $X_{1}|_{\bar{s}}$. The local ring $(\mathcal{O}_{X_{1}, x_{i, s}})_{red}$ is a DVR with
fraction field $K_{i, s}$, and $V_{1}|_{K_{i, s}} = \operatorname{Spec}(\prod_{j \in J_{i, s}} L_{j})$ with $L_{j}$
finite separable over $K_{i, s}$. Let $n_{j, s}$ be the order of the inertia group of a Galois closure of $L_{j}$; let
$n_{i}$ be the lcm of the $n_{j, s}$ as $s$ ranges over the maximal points of $S$ and $j$ over $J_{i, s}$.

#### Uniqueness of the extension.

The smoothness of $X'_{1}$ over $S$ at the points of $Y$ (XIII.5.4) gives $prof_{\acute{e}}t(Y'_{1}, X'_{1}) \geq 2$
(SGA 4 XVI 3.2 or SGA 2 XIV 1.19). The étale-cover-extension theorem at depth $\geq 2$ gives uniqueness.

#### Existence at maximal points of $Y'_{1}$.

Let $\bar{x}'_{1}$ be a geometric point of $Y'_{1} = V(T_{1}) \cup \cdots \cup V(T_{r})$ above a maximal point of
$Y'_{1}$. Write $\bar{X}'_{1}$ for the strict localisation at $\bar{x}'_{1}$. Then $\bar{X}'_{1}|_{red}$ is the spectrum
of a DVR (the local ring at the generic point of a single component $V(T_{i_{0}})$). The étale cover $\bar{V}'_{1}$ of
$\bar{U}'_{1}$ is tame at that DVR (by construction of $n_{i}$), so $\bar{V}'_{1}$ extends to $\bar{X}'_{1}$ by file 51
X.3.6.

#### Extension across the remaining codimensions.

We have an extension over an open $W'_{1} \subseteq X'_{1}$ containing the maximal points of $Y'_{1}$. Set
$Z'_{1} = X'_{1} - W'_{1}$. For $s$ a maximal point of $S$, $codim(Z'_{1}|_{s}, X'_{1}|_{s}) \geq 2$; for $s$ a
non-maximal point, $codim(Z'_{1}|_{s}, X'_{1}|_{s}) \geq 1$ and $prof_{\acute{e}}t_{s}(S) \geq 1$. By SGA 2 XIV 1.20,
the extension across $Z'_{1}$ is automatic.

#### The $n_{i}$ are prime to $p$.

Same contradiction argument as in XIII.5.2: if $p | n_{i_{0}}$, replace $n_{i_{0}}$ by $n_{i_{0}} / p$ and argue that
the extension forces $n_{i_{0}} = 1$, contradicting $p | n_{i_{0}}$. The henselian-descent step uses radicial fibre +
strict- local henselianity, exactly as in XIII.5.2.

This completes the proof of XIII.5.5.

## §XIII.5.6 — Universal tame covering of a strict-local NC neighbourhood relative to $S$

<!-- source: XIII.5.6 -->

### XIII.5.6 — Corollary.

<!-- source: XIII.5.6 -->

Let $X$ be an $S$-scheme, $D = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq r} div(f_{i})$ an NC divisor relative to $S$ as in XIII.5.4. Let
$\bar{x}$ be a geometric point of $X$, $\bar{X}$ the strict localisation at $\bar{x}$, $\bar{Y} = Y \times_{X} \bar{X}$,
$\bar{U} = \bar{X} - \bar{Y}$, and

```text
Ũ  :=  lim_{(n_i)}  Ū [T_i]_{i ∈ I(x)} / (T_i^{n_i} − f_i),
```

the projective limit over $(n_{i})$ prime to the residual characteristic $p$ of $\kappa(x)$. Then `Ũ` is a **universal
tame covering of `Ū` relative to $S$**, and

```text
π_1^tame(Ū)  ≃  ∏_{ℓ ≠ p}  ℤ_ℓ(1)^{I(x)}       (canonical iso).
```

Non-canonically, $\pi^{tame}_{1}(\bar{U}) \simeq (\prod_{\ell \neq p} \mathbb{Z}_{\ell})^{I(x)}$.

*Proof.* XIII.5.5 says every connected tame étale cover of `Ū` arises by quotient from a Kummer cover; passing to the
limit gives the universal property and identifies the automorphism group as in XIII.5.3.

### XIII.5.6.1 — Stratification of $X$ by intersections of boundary components.

<!-- source: XIII.5.6.1 -->

For every subset $I \subseteq [1, r]$, put

```text
X_I  =  (⋂_{i ∈ I} V(f_i))  ∩  (⋂_{i ∉ I} X_{f_i}).
```

The `X_I` form a stratification of $X$ by locally closed subschemes; XIII.5.6 says $\pi^{tame}_{1}$ at a stratum `X_I`
is $\prod_{\ell \neq p} \mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1)^{I}$, one $\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}(1)$ factor per component of the boundary the
point sees. This is the *local model* of the tame fundamental group along an NC divisor in arbitrary dimension.

## Closing of the gentle SGA project

The gentle SGA pass through SGA 1 is complete with file 64. The thirteen exposés covered are:

- **Exposé I (étale morphisms).** Files 10–12 (with the preparation arc 01–06 and prep file 23).
- **Exposé II (smooth morphisms).** Files 14–16.
- **Exposé III (extension properties of smooth morphisms; lifting).** Files 19–22 (with prep files 17–18).
- **Exposé IV (flat morphisms).** Files 24–27.
- **Exposé V (the fundamental group: generalities).** Files 30–33 (with prep files 28–29).
- **Exposé VI (fibred categories and descent).** Files 35–38 (with prep file 34).
- **Exposé VIII (descent of quasi-coherent modules and morphisms).** Files 40–43.
- **Exposé IX (descent of étale morphisms; effectivity; Stein factorisation).** Files 44–47.
- **Exposé X (Specialisation of $\pi_{1}$; the transcendental method; Abhyankar).** Files 48–51.
- **Exposé XI (Projective spaces, abelian varieties, principal bundles, Kummer / Artin–Schreier).** Files 52–56.
- **Exposé XII (analytic geometry, GAGA, Riemann existence).** Files 57–60.
- **Exposé XIII (cohomological properness).** Files 61–64.

The single exposé of SGA 1 *not* given a gentle pass is **Exposé VII** (la descente fidèlement plate des revêtements).
The étale-fundamental-group story of Exposé V (file 33) and the cohomological-properness story of Exposés IX and XIII
together cover the material readers most often want from VII; SGA 1 VII itself is short and mostly soritic.

#### What we did *not* prove.

- **SGA 4 itself** — the full étale-cohomology framework. Files 61–64 use it as a standing bridge (file 61 §XIII.B) and
  quote its main statements as black boxes.
- **SGA 5 II proper base change in higher dimension.** Same status as SGA 4.
- **SGA 7 application to proper smooth surfaces.** Pointer at the end of file 63 (XIII.4.8).
- **Abhyankar's conjecture in characteristic $p$.** Pointer at XIII.2.13; proved by Raynaud / Harbater.
- **Strong desingularization in characteristic $p$.** Pointer to Hironaka in characteristic zero; the characteristic-
  $p$ analogue is open. The Künneth formula XIII.4.6 is therefore unconditional in characteristic zero and depends on a
  desingularization hypothesis in characteristic $p$.

#### Forward path.

Reader who has worked through files 01–64 is ready to:

- read **SGA 4** in full (étale topology and étale cohomology, with the gentle SGA series providing the geometric
  intuition for $\pi^{\acute{e}}_{1}t$, fibre products, descent, NC divisors, and Riemann existence);
- read **SGA 4½** (Deligne, *Cohomologie étale*) and the Weil conjectures;
- read **SGA 5** (cohomologically proper morphisms in dim `> 1`, trace formula, ℓ-adic Lefschetz);
- read **SGA 7** (Murre's surface-$\pi_{1}$ calculation, vanishing-cycles framework);
- read modern reader's-companion accounts: J. Milne, *Étale Cohomology* (Princeton, 1980); J.-P. Serre's surveys on
  $\pi_{1}$; Lenstra, *Galois Theory for Schemes*; Murre / Cadoret, *Galois Categories*; Vistoli, *FGA Explained*
  (descent and stacks); Stacks Project.

#### Final slogan.

**The gentle SGA series builds the étale fundamental group, the descent framework, the analytic comparison, and the
cohomological-properness language from rings and categories up — every theorem stated exactly, every black box named,
every cross-reference to a file in this series. It ends where SGA 4 begins.**
